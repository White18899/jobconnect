Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview

JobWay ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates a direct verified employment network for software engineers, QA testers, and tech leaders across Visakhapatnam and India. We are committed to transparency and safeguarding user privacy in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, apply for vacancies, or publish employment positions on JobWay, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, verified email address, and mobile phone number for authentication and OTP verification.
  • Professional Credentials: Primary technical trade, years of experience, skill tags, GitHub/LinkedIn profile URLs, and uploaded resumes/CVs (PDF/DOCX).
  • Employer Information: Business legal name, company registration ID, GSTIN/official email, and contact person details.
  • Transaction References: 12-digit UPI reference (UTR) numbers and screenshot proofs for employer credit recharge verification.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To match candidates with verified corporate vacancies without recruitment agency brokerage.
  • To enforce fair use: Candidate direct phone numbers are protected behind authenticated contact unlock credits to prevent unsolicited spam.
  • To deliver security notifications, job application alerts, and OTP authentication codes.
  • We never sell, broker, or rent candidate or employer personal data to third-party telemarketers or external advertising platforms.

4. Data Security & Storage

JobWay is hosted on enterprise-grade Cloudflare infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers, D1 encrypted SQL database, and R2 private object storage). All data transmissions are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain complete ownership of your personal data. You may update your profile information anytime via your dashboard. To request permanent account deletion and erasure of your uploaded documents from our servers, contact our support team at support@job-way.in.
